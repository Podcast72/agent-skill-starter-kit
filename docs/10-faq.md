# FAQ

## Q: Is this a framework?

A: No. It is a starter kit and reference structure.

## Q: Do I need scripts?

A: No. A basic skill can be only `SKILL.md`.

## Q: When should I add scripts?

A: When a task is repeatable, mechanical or easy to check.

## Q: Do scripts replace the agent?

A: No. Scripts support repeatable work. The agent still reasons and decides.

## Q: Are hooks required?

A: No. Hooks are optional and should be used carefully.

## Q: Is the experimental passive skill self-improving?

A: No. It only records minimal metadata for manual review.

## Q: Can I use this with my own naming?

A: Yes. Rename folders, skill names and prompts as needed.

## Q: What should I avoid putting in a public skill?

A: Secrets, private paths, credentials, personal data, proprietary logic and unsafe commands.
